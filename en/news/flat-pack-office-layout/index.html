How Can Flat-Pack Containers Become an Efficient Office?

The value of a flat-pack container is not limited to one room. Like building blocks, modules can be organised into an office environment that fits the way a project team works.

CONTENTS01　Begin with people and movement02　Use combinations to solve scale03　Let the fit-out follow the use

Begin with people and movement

Project management, engineering, document control, meetings and reception have different working patterns. The layout should distinguish quiet work areas, shared collaboration space and visitor-facing areas.

The position of entrances, corridors and stairs also affects everyday efficiency, so crossing flows should be kept to a minimum.

Use combinations to solve scale

Parallel modules can form meeting rooms, open offices and reception spaces. Two-level combinations increase usable area on a limited site, while corridors and canopies improve movement in wet weather.

The modular approach also allows the office to expand as the project develops instead of requiring every space on day one.

Let the fit-out follow the use

Lighting, sockets, network, air conditioning, partitions and storage determine whether an office is genuinely useful. Confirming these details during planning reduces later rework.
